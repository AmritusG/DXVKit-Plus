## Step 3 — every candidate in the shared path, one at a time, each with a falsifying measurement
1. Forward/inverse YCoCg-R rounding — CAUSE (shared, encoder). Exhaustive over all 16,777,216 RGB:
   encoder forward in code values, x4, decoder inverse in quarter units, (v+2)>>2: 4,194,304 colours
   (25.0%) wrong, all with odd Co and odd Cg, R and B exactly 1 low, G never. The decoder's own
   quarter-unit forward (core::lmbx write_direct): 0 wrong. Present at any quality: v1 at quality 1
   (87 KB, card) decodes (80,140,255) -0.98 / -0.01 / -0.98 and (255,120,0) -0.99 R.
2. 10-bit quarter-code output and (v+2)>>2 — not a cause: with the exact forward the full chain is
   lossless over all 16.7M colours (item 1, 0 wrong).
3. Clamping of out-of-gamut YCoCg — not a cause: the shift hits unsaturated colours as hard as
   saturated ones (background (18,20,32): -6.9 R); the lattice prediction (item 4, clamping
   included) matches every flat colour, and none of them clamps.
4. DC step floors (2 Y/A, 4 Co/Cg) and DC prediction — CAUSE, but not the floors: the DC step grows

- A static quantiser cap on smooth blocks (<= 6): flat errors 0.10, but the card's rate floor rose
  to 6.8 KB (LMB7 5.8 KB). Replaced by the chain-exact DC guard (+0 B at the floor).
- A region = a colour covering >= 32 tile pixels: anti-aliased edge lines counted as regions, lossy
  palettes were rejected on text tiles, eye frame 48.4 -> 41.3 dB at LMB7's bytes. Replaced by
  2x2-cell regions.

  (11,059 B / 51.05 dB against 0.6.0's 10,960 B / 55.19) — removed.
- Pinned Lloyd seeds: superseded by region-mean centroids, which also let near-equal regions merge.

  chroma): glow 4:2:2 +19% bytes at equal q, -0.45 dB at LMB7's bytes. Fixed: keep the step.
