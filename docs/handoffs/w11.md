# W11: ship rate-distortion steps (0.7.1)

Shipped from main, tagged `v0.7.1`. This is an encoder-only change: no bitstream change and no decoder change (the diff in the core, wgpu and apple crates is empty).

## What shipped
- **Rate-distortion quantiser steps** in the LMBX encoder, on by default.
  - In 4:4:4 frames with tile modes, each 8x8 block of a DCT tile takes the step of least `J` among three: the AC-energy rule's step and the one either side.
  - `J` = AC error over every plane, with v3's weights, + lambda × AC bits.
  - The step is re-chosen on every RDO pass against that pass's rate model. The colour guard runs afterwards, as before.
  - Tiles of at most 64 colours and 4:2:2 frames keep 0.7.0's steps.
  - `--rd-steps off` (`LmbxParams::rd_steps = false`) is the 0.7.0 encoder, byte for byte.
- **A ported copy of W10's control arm** (`--tsizes rd8` on `w10-explore`), not new logic. It writes the same bytes as W10's control arm: every A and ladder row is identical, and the 30-frame glow clip is byte-identical.
- **Rate-control safety net.** With RD steps on, the bisection continues to 1/64 while its best frame is more than 3% under the budget.
  - Reason: the rule's step walls left the quality tool's fit at 640 KB of 753 KB on glow.
  - It never fired on the 30 glow frames.
  - With `off`, rate control is 0.7.0's.
- **Docs:** `docs/LMBD.md` §11.11 (informative encoder text), the README release line, a feature bullet and table, and `CHANGELOG.md` 0.7.1.
- **Tooling:** the `lmbx_quality` example gains a `ladder` command and a `rule` arm. Version bumped to 0.7.1.
- **Vectors.** The two 4:4:4 plasma vectors' re-encode hashes are re-pinned. The committed vector files are unchanged, and every graphics vector re-encodes identically.

## Deviations
- **4:4:4 only.** RD steps apply to 4:4:4 frames only, which is what W10 measured; 4:2:2 is unchanged. In 4:2:2 a chroma block's step is carried by its even luma block, and that was not measured.
- **Rate-control refinement added.** It was not in W10. It is gated on `rd_steps`, so `off` stays 0.7.0's encoder exactly.
- **Synthetic alpha costs −0.06 dB RGB** at 0.7% fewer bytes. This is the only content that does not gain.
- **Timings are from a shared machine.** Relative numbers only.
